## About Me

I'm a Computer Science Teacher at the [Military Lyceum of the National Army Academy](https://vlnasv.mil.gov.ua/) and a programming instructor at [IT-Elit](https://www.instagram.com/it_elit_school/).

I combine education, software development, artificial intelligence, and digital creativity.

I enjoy building practical digital tools, educational platforms, web applications, and interactive projects.

I also work with FPV drones and teach students the fundamentals of FPV piloting at the Military Lyceum.
